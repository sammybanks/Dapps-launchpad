Frequently asked questions

What makes your Web3 solutions "unhackable"

We use audited smart contracts, zero-trust architecture, and battle-tested encryption—combining decentralized design with enterprise-grade security protocols to minimize attack vectors.

How is "trustless" security different from traditional security?

Trustless systems (like blockchain) remove reliance on intermediaries. Our solutions leverage cryptographic proofs and self-custody tools, so users control their assets without trusting third parties.

What chains does dApp support?

dApp supports Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Binance Smart Chain, Avalanche, Scroll, Linea, Blast, Mode and Mantle.

Who uses your Web3 gateway?

From DeFi startups (needing hack-resistant smart contracts) to enterprises (seeking private blockchain tools), we tailor solutions for scalability and compliance.
